Frequently asked questions

Prerequisites

What are the prerequisites?

The course has no formal prerequisites, like all courses at IITGN. It assumes a basic understanding of programming in Python.

What is the prerequisite exam?

An exam we designed to make sure you have the background needed for the course.

How can I prepare for the prerequisite exam?

Your UG course material should be enough to prepare for it.

What happens if I do not clear the prerequisite exam?

You will not be allowed to take the course.

What does "clear" the exam mean?

As in all courses at IITGN, the instructor decides the cut-off, and you have to score above it. The cut-off is not revealed to students in advance.

Laptop policy

Can I use my laptop or phone in class?

No. You may not use your laptop or phone in class for any reason, unless it is a lab session where the instructor has explicitly allowed laptops.

Quizzes

What happens if I miss a quiz for any reason?
  • The quiz is marked 0.
  • The best 3 of 4 rule is designed with such cases in mind.
Are the quizzes and the end-semester exam open book? Can I bring notes?

No. The exams and quizzes are closed book, and you may not bring notes.

How soon do I get my answer sheets back?

Expect them back in 4 to 5 working days.

Is there a mid-semester or end-semester exam?

2 of the 4 quizzes are held in the mid-semester and end-semester slots. The other two are held during the semester.

Are the quizzes multiple choice or written?

A quiz may have both multiple-choice and written questions.

Assignments

What happens if I miss an assignment for any reason?

There are no extensions for assignments.

I have a doubt about an assignment. Whom should I write to?

Ask on the Slack #assignments channel. If you get no response within 2 days, write to the course instructor.

I do not know Python. Can I write the assignments in another language?

No. You have to use Python.

How are assignments evaluated?

Each assignment is followed by a viva. The TAs first check the code against the submission; any change from the submitted code is not allowed, and any instance of it leads to a warning. The TAs then run the code and ask a few questions: about 75% on the assignment itself and about 25% on the theory behind the concepts it covers.

Assignment marks Share
Code runs correctly and solves the problem 50%
Questions on the assignment and your understanding of the code 25%
Code quality 12.5%
Questions on the theory behind the concepts in the assignment 12.5%
Are assignments individual or in groups?

In groups. All members of a team get the same grade for the assignment.

Attendance

What is the attendance policy?

Attendance is set out in the grading policy.

Audit policy

Can I audit the course?

You are free to attend the lectures. However, with a limited number of TAs and a large number of students, we cannot grade your submissions, and you cannot formally contribute to any team's assignments.

Computational resources

Will we get cloud credits?

Typically we have been kindly given Google Cloud credits. We will update this section once we have them. The credits are more than enough for the assignments.
