Lecture library

Every lecture of ES 114 Probability, statistics and data visualization, January to April 2025, with its slides, notebooks, cheat sheet and short videos.

Each row is one lecture, grouped by part of the course. The notebooks are from psdv-teaching, which has the source code for the lectures and all rendered notebooks. Recordings of the tutorials are in a YouTube playlist. The Shorts are also collected, in course order, on the Probability and statistics in 1 minute page and in a YouTube playlist.

Slides: chapter slides from Introduction to Probability for Data Science and handwritten notes from class (PDF) · Notebooks: worked code, or a Colab notebook for a lab · Apps: interactive demos · Cheat sheet: a two-page A4 summary (PDF) · Shorts: one-minute videos, each answering one question; point at a number to see the question · Videos: explainers by others (point at one to see its author) · Recording: a tutorial video by the instructor.
